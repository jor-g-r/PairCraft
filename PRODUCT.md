# Paircraft

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People with a bottle in mind deciding what to cook or order. Wine → food is the primary journey, confirmed by Jorge on 2026-09-25. The next demo audience remains Teo and the CUHELAV profesora; B2B versus B2C positioning awaits their feedback.

## Product Purpose

A pocket guide linking wines, dishes, grapes and regions. Success is finding a useful pairing and understanding why in one short sentence.

## Operating Context

Mobile-first, at home, at a restaurant or in a wine shop. Browse a local-market feed, find a bottle, open its pairings, follow a dish. Catalogs and contextual links support discovery.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Astro Content Collections hold curated entities and prose. A deterministic engine supplies qualitative pairing tiers. Runtime AI only routes free-text dishes to curated dishes; it does not invent wine recommendations or scores. Jorge approved the dedicated /pair route on 2026-09-25, superseding the earlier demo-dependent deferral for this feature. Tier tuning remains gated on demo feedback. No accounts or payments in this iteration.

## Brand Commitments

Paircraft identity; concise, opinionated pocket-guide voice. EN/ES with honest per-field fallback. User-supplied Merlot light/dark wireframes establish the visual reference. Theme toggle, top navigation and per-theme wine-red accents are confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand

The committed corpus is the source of wine facts and pairing prose. The supplied stock photo is Matthias Oberholzer's Unsplash image zmH_rmAxOgE. Missing sensory data must not be invented. No demo feedback or customer testimonials exist yet.

## Product Principles

- Bottle first, food next.
- Explain a recommendation briefly.
- Keep origin distinct from retail availability.
- Let every entity lead to a useful next step.
- Use real corpus data; expose gaps honestly.
