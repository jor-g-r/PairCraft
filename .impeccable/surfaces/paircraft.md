# Paircraft UX expansion

Mode: Operate on catalogs and /pair; Read on entity pages and About.
Authority: Jorge's approved plan and Merlot light/dark reference PDFs, 2026-09-25.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A bottle opens a menu of possibilities. Recommendations are easy to reach before deeper sensory reading.

OWN-WORLD: White or charcoal ground, Playfair names, Open Sans controls, wine-red verdicts. A compact dark serving panel contrasts with oval property panels; ruled headings and thin sensory tracks echo the supplied wireframe.

STORY: Find the bottle, read its character, choose a dish, understand the match. Return to the catalog without losing filters. /pair is a secondary entry into curated dish pages with explicit exact/related matches.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two-row mobile header, compact wayfinding, centered wine identity and tagline, direct pairing jump. Sensory properties follow in a 2×2 mobile grid and four columns on desktop. Pairings use readable rows on mobile and three columns on desktop.

FORM: User-pinned Merlot reference; no open identity selection. Light and dark are for bright shopping contexts and evening tables, with a persisted manual override.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
