Venezuela grows no wine at scale — the market is entirely import, and import
constraints keep the range narrow and value-driven. Chilean and Argentine reds
dominate (Cabernet, Malbec, everyday brands like Gato Negro), with Spanish
bottles rounding out the shelf.
