Rías Baixas means "lower estuaries" — the fingers of Atlantic that cut
into Galicia's coast. The cool wet climate is the opposite of what most
people picture when they think Spain, and that's the point. Without the
Atlantic moderation, Albariño wouldn't have its acid spine.

Five sub-zones, but Val do Salnés is the heart — closest to the sea,
most saline, most Albariño-dominant.
