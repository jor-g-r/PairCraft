The Veneto stretches from the Dolomites to the Adriatic, covering more DOC ground than anywhere else in Italy. That scale is the problem and the opportunity. Soave's volcanic basalt, Valpolicella's limestone ridges, and Prosecco's steep Conegliano-Valdobbiadene hillsides are genuinely distinct terroirs buried under an avalanche of industrial lookalikes.

The stylistic signature splits hard: Amarone delivers dried-grape concentration and iron-fisted tannin that demands brasato or ribeye, charred; Soave Classico at its best runs lean and mineral, built for salt-cod or grilled branzino. Buy Classico or single-vineyard. Producers worth trusting: Quintarelli, Dal Forno, Allegrini in Valpolicella; Pieropan, Gini in Soave. Prosecco Superiore DOCG only — skip the DOC floor wine.
