Mendoza is the Andes translated into wine. The altitude does what cool
climate does elsewhere — it keeps the acid alive, slows the sugar curve,
holds the perfume in the grape. Without it, this would be raisin country.

The region is large enough to be a continent of styles: Maipú warm and
generous, Luján de Cuyo classical, the Uco Valley high and tense. Malbec
is the through-line; everything else is supporting cast.
