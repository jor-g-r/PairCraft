Somontano sits at the foot of the Aragonese Pyrenees, a small DO that planted Cabernet, Merlot, Syrah, and Chardonnay in the 1980s when the rest of Spain was still arguing about Tempranillo. The gamble paid off. Limestone and clay soils at 400–700 metres give the wines a mineral backbone that keeps the fruit honest.

Reds lead here: Cabernet and Syrah show dark fruit with a savory, iron-edged finish — tannin meets protein, full stop. Whites from Chardonnay and local Alcañón run lean and citric, acid cuts fat. Viñas del Vero and Enate built the region's reputation; they still set the benchmark. Buy the reds with confidence.
